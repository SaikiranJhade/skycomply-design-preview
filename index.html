What is locked

Header, sidebar, breadcrumbs and footer are generated from one shell in _shell.mjs, so every screen is byte-identical outside its content region. Nav order mirrors shared/rbac/registry.ts.

Sample data only

No resident PHI. Residents are placeholders such as Resident A, and every survey id, case number, community and staff name is invented.

Known token issue

In the light theme --accent-11 is the brand amber and fails contrast as text (1.59:1 on accent-3). See the README for the measurement and the one-line fix.
