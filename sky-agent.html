How should I word the monitoring step for F-880 at Cedar Ridge?
SJ

Your infection control policy IC-114 requires a twice-daily isolation cart check logged by the charge nurse. A monitoring step that matches both the policy and the surveyor's expectation reads:

The Infection Preventionist will audit 5 isolation setups weekly for 4 weeks, then monthly for 3 months. Results report to the QAPI committee, with re-education for any staff member found out of compliance.

Source: IC-114 Source: 42 CFR 483.80
